About TND

Better context for the news you read.

TND is an AI-assisted news analysis tool built to help you look beyond the headline.

It examines an article for signals such as potentially unsupported claims, sensational language, source information, and other indicators that may warrant further verification.

TND doesn't tell you what to believe. Instead, it gives you context and signals to help you make a more informed judgment.

“Think of TND as a second look — not the final word.”

A useful boundary

What TND Doesn't Do

TND does not guarantee that an article is true or false. AI-assisted analysis can make mistakes, miss context, or interpret information incorrectly.

For important decisions, always consult the original source and reliable independent sources.

Use TND as a starting point for verification — not as a replacement for it.
